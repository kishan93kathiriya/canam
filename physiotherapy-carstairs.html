Frequently Asked Questions

Is Can-Am Physical Therapy located in Carstairs?
No. Can-Am's physical clinic is located at 818 Railway Street in Crossfield. The clinic welcomes patients travelling from Carstairs and nearby communities.
Do you provide home physiotherapy in Carstairs?
Can-Am offers orthopedic home-care appointments for eligible Carstairs addresses within the clinic's 10–20 km service range. Exact distance and availability are confirmed when booking.
What conditions can physiotherapy help with?
Treatment may help with back and neck pain, shoulder problems, hip and knee pain, sports injuries, strains, sprains, reduced mobility and post-surgical recovery. Your physiotherapist will assess your needs before recommending a plan.
Does the clinic accept MVA and WCB cases?
Yes. Can-Am accepts eligible motor vehicle accident and WCB cases and provides dedicated initial assessment options through JaneApp.
How long is an initial physiotherapy appointment?
A standard clinic-based initial assessment and treatment is scheduled for 45 minutes. An initial assessment involving two body areas and an orthopedic home-care evaluation are scheduled for 60 minutes.
Is direct billing available?
Direct billing is available. Coverage and reimbursement rules vary by plan, so patients should confirm their individual benefits and any referral requirements with their insurer.
