How we help you recover

1

Full assessment

Common questions

Questions from Airdrie patients

Do you see patients from Airdrie?

Yes. We welcome patients from Airdrie, Balzac and nearby communities at our clinic at 818 Railway St in Crossfield.

Do you accept MVA claims?

Yes. MVA claims are accepted and direct billing is available. Call us if you have questions about your claim before you book.

My pain started a few days after the accident. Should I still book?

Yes. Neck stiffness, headaches and back pain can take hours or days to show up after a collision. If something does not feel right, book an assessment.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring photo ID, the date of the accident, and your claim and adjuster details if you have them. Wear comfortable clothing so we can assess the areas that hurt.
