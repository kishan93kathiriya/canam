Who we are

A personal approach to moving well

Can-Am Physical Therapy & Rehab is built on a simple idea: everyone deserves to move freely and confidently, regardless of age or ability. Our Crossfield physiotherapists combine hands-on treatment with science-backed exercise to restore function, strength and control, whether you visit the clinic, book a home visit or are recovering after an accident, injury or surgery.

Know Your Therapists

One-on-one assessment and rehabilitation at our Crossfield clinic.

Ankit Patel

Physiotherapist

Ankit has worked in the fitness and rehabilitation industry for many years. He brings a wealth of diverse experience, having worked in gym settings, with young athletes, the Olympic speed skaters' team, and ballet dancers. His career spans various environments including nursing homes, home care, prisons, and specialized clinics.

He established his own clinic to prioritize exceptional manual therapy and provide a dedicated one-on-one, hands-on experience for every client. His primary goal is to help patients gain functional independence and significantly improve their quality of life.

Clinical Expertise
Advanced Manual Therapy IASTM & Cupping Therapy Active Release Technique Dry Needling Concussion & Vestibular Neuro Rehab (Stroke, MS, ALS) Ortho & Sport Rehab Cardio-respiratory Rehab MVA, WCB & LTD Claims

Gursharanjit Bhambra

Physiotherapist, Pelvic Health

Sharan believes good physiotherapy starts with listening: understanding what you are experiencing, what is important to you, and what you want to feel confident doing again.

Her experience includes pelvic health, musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation, vestibular care, functional dry needling, exercise prescription and sports injury rehabilitation. She enjoys working one-on-one with patients and creating care that feels personal, comfortable and easy to understand.

Her goal is to help you feel heard and supported throughout your care, working together to improve movement, function and confidence so you can return to the activities and everyday moments that matter to you.

Areas of Focus
Pelvic Health Functional Dry Needling Exercise Prescription Sports Injury Rehabilitation Vestibular Rehabilitation

Kinjal Laddha

Physiotherapist

Kinjal is an internationally trained physiotherapist with over five years of clinical experience, including three years internationally and two years in Canada. After graduating in India in 2020, she completed a Post-Graduate Diploma in Fitness and Health Promotion at Humber College in Toronto, along with certifications in Diet and Nutrition and Yoga Training.

Her approach combines hands-on techniques like soft tissue release and joint mobilization with evidence-based exercise prescription. She also has experience with Functional Capacity Evaluations (FCEs) and Work Conditioning Programs that support a safe return to work.

Outside the clinic, you'll find Kinjal hiking and biking in the mountains, swimming, playing badminton, or spending time at the beach and lakes with friends and family.

Areas of Focus
Soft Tissue Release Joint Mobilization Exercise Prescription Functional Capacity Evaluations Work Conditioning Return-to-Work Programs
